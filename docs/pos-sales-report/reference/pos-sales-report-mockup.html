What the report contains

  • Rows one per POS order line (sales and refunds), from paid, posted and invoiced orders. Draft and cancelled orders are left out.
  • Columns Date, Order, Session, Point of Sale, Employee, Customer, Product, Category, Qty, Unit price, Discount, Net (excl. tax), Tax, Total, and Margin for POS administrators.
  • Amounts in company currency, refunds negative, figures identical to Point of Sale › Reporting › Orders (same source, Odoo's standard POS analysis).
  • Payments printed as a separate table from the actual payments, because one order can be paid with several methods. Payment method is a filter, not a line column.
  • Header of the PDF company, period, every active filter and grouping in words, who printed it and when, page x of y.
