Orders workspace

The order-import command list. Every Shopify order the connector tracks, with its Shopify financial state, Shopify fulfillment summary, connector state, and the confirmation-policy outcome. All eight financial states are represented across the rows. Roles: the Connector User works this list; the Connector Administrator sets the policies that produce the outcome column. States: loaded, loading, empty, error, manual-review emphasis, unknown-schema warning.
