Product export flow

The controlled Odoo→Shopify product export: select explicitly, preview a fresh-read field diff (merchant-owned fields locked and structurally absent from the payload), confirm the exact diff, watch per-record progress, and read honest results — including uncertain outcomes that are decided by reconciliation reads, never blind retries. Exports create Shopify products as DRAFT/unpublished; publishing is a separate explicit step. Visual prototype only — no RPC or field contract is invented.
