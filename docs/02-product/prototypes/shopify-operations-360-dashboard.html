  1. An administrator enables order import in Store Settings.
  2. The first scan discovers orders inside the configured import window (30 days by default; Shopify grants access to orders older than 60 days only with the read_all_orders scope).

PROTOTYPE ANNOTATION — the three empty-state variants
1 · No sales in this period Connector healthy and data current, but zero orders in the window: the sales cards show €0.00 / 0 / — / 0 with the bridge reading “Complete & current”, and the trend area says “No sales in this period.” Nothing is dimmed or alarmed.
2 · Sales data not synchronized yet (shown above) Order import disabled or never run: no fake zeros — the sales region is replaced by the guided card, while connector health below stays fully visible.
3 · Historical coverage uncertain, or a permissions limit When the selected period reaches further back than the connector can vouch for: “The selected period may include dates earlier than the connector’s available imported history (import window 30 days; orders older than 60 days need Shopify’s read_all_orders approval).” No cutoff date is ever shown — the earliest imported order proves nothing about coverage, and no stored boundary exists today (exact boundary detection is a recorded backend enhancement). If the viewer’s role cannot read sales amounts: “Your role can’t read sales amounts — connector health is unaffected.” Never rendered as €0.00.
