# Grouped root causes and fixes — completion pass 25 September 2026

Each root cause was found in the real Odoo 19 dashboard (local disposable database, demo data plus
Sales, Purchase, Inventory, CRM, Time Off and Attendances) with the full control sweep, the paired
comparison against the approved HTML, or the targeted browser checks. Every fix has a regression test
that fails on the previous code and passes on the candidate, unless the row says otherwise.

| ID | Root cause | Visible symptoms (all departments checked) | Fix | Regression test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RC1 | A scope change cleared lazily loaded workspace lists but reloaded only some of them. | After Refresh, a period change or a company switch: Sales delivery panel stuck on "Loading…"; Inventory Products & locations table disappeared and its Valuation/Forecast buttons did nothing; Procurement worklist disappeared. | `refresh()` reloads the visible department through one `loadSectionData()` helper shared with navigation; the Procurement worklist mode survives a same-company refresh. A return from a native record by breadcrumb also loads the restored department's lists that were not saved (review M1: CRM and Procurement records stayed on "Loading records…"). | `frontend.test.mjs`: *refresh and company changes reload the visible workspace lists…*, *returning from a native record reloads the restored department lists…*. Browser: breadcrumb return from a CRM and a Procurement record. |
| RC2 | The dashboard had no addressable state, and the URL writer used a full-state replace. | Links used raw numeric action IDs; the department, HR tab and dates were lost on reload; the first implementation of URL sync dropped the action itself (`/odoo?section=sales`), so a reload opened the Odoo home. | Action `path` `executive-dashboard`; `router.replaceState()` (merge, no new history entry) keeps the validated department, HR tab and non-default dates. Unknown or invalid URL values are ignored. A department the user picks during the first load wins over the URL (review m1); the URL is corrected when a company switch hides the current department (n2); inside an Odoo dialog the URL is left alone (`env.inDialog`, m2). | `test_dashboard.py`: *test_dashboard_action_has_a_portable_url_path*; `frontend.test.mjs`: URL tests with an Odoo-faithful router mock, *a department chosen during the first load wins…*, *…also corrects the URL*, *the URL is left alone … inside a dialog*. Browser: reload, Back from a native report, invalid-input checks. |
| RC3 | Unavailable states still offered actions, and some used generic wording. | Chart Retry called a finance-addon method that does not exist without that addon (server AttributeError); empty finance groups showed a blank area; HR showed "App not installed" instead of naming Attendances, Time Off or Planning (addendum B12). | The chart names the blocking status and offers Retry only for real load errors; the Finance section Retry requests the chart and cash accounts only when their metrics are ready, as a refresh does (review m3); empty groups explain why; HR states name the missing standard app on every tab, Overview card, preview and profile. | `frontend.test.mjs`: *chartBlockedReason*, *finance Retry requests the chart and cash accounts only when…* and *missing HR sources name their standard app…* |
| RC4 | Filter choices depended on the order in which HR tabs were opened. | A tab opened directly (link, reload, saved view) offered only "All departments" and "Unassigned". | Every filterable HR tab response carries the authorized department choices (same HR-officer boundary as the Overview team list; a denial gives no choices). | `test_hr_workspace.py`: department choices per tab and withheld for a reader without HR rights. |
| RC5 | Copy drifted from the approved HTML. | Four department subtitles differed from the approved text; card hints started with lowercase fragments; the reference-style cash card printed "active accounts" without a count when the directory was not loaded. | Approved subtitles restored (Arabic added); sentence-case hints; "Active accounts" fallback. | Browser: *department subtitles match the approved HTML*, *card hints are sentences*. |
| RC6 | Menu and keyboard behaviour were incomplete (UI04). | The More menu stayed open on outside press, Escape lost focus, arrow keys did nothing and the menu could overflow at 390 px. | Outside press closes; Escape returns focus to More; Arrow/Home/End move between items; the first item receives focus; the menu flips inside the viewport (English and Arabic). | `frontend.test.mjs` menu tests; browser checks at 1440 and 390, English and Arabic. |
| RC7 | Formatting differed from the approved cards. | Headline cards showed cents; ranking initials could include reference brackets or digits; quantity rankings used currency formatting. | Whole currency units on headline cards (exact value in the tooltip and drawers); letters-only initials (digits dropped too, review n1: "3M Company" is "MC"); quantity formatting for quantity rankings. | `frontend.test.mjs` headline, initials and ranking tests. |
| RC8 | Company identity and selectors did not handle long names or right-to-left text. | "My Company (San Francisco)" wrapped to four lines in the rail; on phones the company selector was capped at 160 px and, in Arabic, showed the end of an English name. | Names over 18 characters stack under the logo; company selectors use `dir="auto"`, an ellipsis and, on phones, the whole row. HR's period bar now has the same native-backed company selector as the approved design, with its dropdown arrow (review m4: Odoo's global select style had removed it). | Browser checks at 1440 and 390, English and Arabic (visual evidence); computed-style check of the arrow. |
| RC9 | Deprecated Odoo 19 API and missing packaging assets. | Every RPC used `check_field_access_rights` (deprecated in 19.0); `/web/manifest.webmanifest` referenced a missing module icon (404 on every page) and the Enterprise home menu had no app icon. | `check_readable()` delegates to `_check_field_access`, the hook `read()` itself enforces; packaged SVG/PNG icon and menu `web_icon`. | `test_dashboard.py`: *test_readable_field_check_keeps_deprecated_semantics*, *test_app_menu_has_a_packaged_icon*. |
| RC10 | A standard action was chosen by name, not by its first view. | HR Employees "Open Employees" and the department drill-down opened an empty **new employee** form: `hr.open_view_employee_list` is `form,list` (`hr/views/hr_employee_views.xml`). | Both use the Employees menu action `hr.open_view_employee_list_my` (kanban, list, form) with the dashboard's scoped domain; a record link still opens that employee's form. The other 17 standard actions the dashboard opens were checked: none is form-first. | `test_hr_workspace.py`: *test_employee_list_links_open_existing_employees*. Browser: the link opens the Employees list. |
| RC11 | A company switch reset the HR tab. | Switching company on Attendance, Time off, Shifts or Employees returned to Overview. The approved HTML keeps the tab and resets only filters, pages and drawers. | The visible HR tab is kept; its filters restart from that tab's own defaults (Shifts: published), so no other-company department or employee survives. | `frontend.test.mjs`: *a company switch keeps the HR tab and starts it from that tab's default filters*. |
| RC12 | Text forced onto one line overflowed narrow containers. | HR name cells inherited Bootstrap's no-wrap from `.btn`: at 390 px the employee subtitle (Odoo's full department path) ran up to 260 px past the sticky first column and overlapped the next column on Attendance, Time off and Employees; at 768 px it widened the first column so Dates wrapped one word per line (the approved HTML wraps these subtitles). Between 901 and 1100 px the 160 px rail kept "Search documents" and "Data & definitions" on one line, running into the content (the approved HTML does the same), and in Arabic "البحث في المستندات" overflowed the 184 px rail at 1440 px. | Names and work profiles wrap inside their column (the shared panel-link rule that forced one line no longer wins for HR people); rail labels wrap inside the rail at 1100 px and below and in Arabic (the Arabic rule took effect with RC14), and keep the approved single line in English above 1100 px. | Bounds check (every department and HR tab, English 390/768/1024/1440 and Arabic 390/1440): no content spills over a neighbouring column; paired captures. |
| RC13 | A restore reused the previously applied stock filters. | Restoring a saved view while Inventory was already loaded showed the saved filters in the form but loaded the table with the earlier ones (existing before this pass; found by the independent review). | The restore applies the saved filters the way Apply does, so the form and the table agree. | `frontend.test.mjs`: *restoring a saved view while Inventory is loaded applies the saved stock filters*. |
| RC14 | Right-to-left rules were keyed on `:dir(rtl)`, which never matches in Odoo. | Odoo 19 marks right-to-left pages with the `o_rtl` body class (`web/static/src/start.js`) and sets no `dir` attribute, so three rules never applied in Arabic: the approved heading rule (`[dir=rtl] h1 { letter-spacing:0 }` in the approved HTML), the tracking reset for the other Latin display styles, and the Arabic rail wrapping from RC12 — "البحث في المستندات" still ran 8 px out of its rail button at 1440 px (found by the final bounds check, after the independent review). | Each such rule also matches `.o_rtl`, the form Odoo's own styles and the dashboard's arrow rules already use. | Style only: the Arabic 1440 px bounds check fails on the previous build (10 spills) and passes on the candidate; paired Arabic captures. |
