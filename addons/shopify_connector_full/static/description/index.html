Shopify Connector Full for Odoo 19

Operate the store with guarded write-back domains

The DEC-029 Full edition installs the complete Lite visibility layer and adds controlled inventory, fulfillment/tracking, and product-export modules. Each write-capable domain keeps its own preview, confirmation, readback, and operator-review controls.

Full is a thin meta-application. It adds no models, views, data records, scheduled actions, controllers, credentials, or Shopify requests of its own. The direct domain modules and webhook satellites remain independently identifiable and own their security and lifecycle rules.

The installation closure also includes the generic webhook foundation and separate product, order, inventory, and fulfillment webhook accelerators. Supported events enter existing asynchronous read jobs; scheduled reconciliation remains the loss-recovery backstop. No timing or exactly-once promise is made.

Included edition scope

  • Everything in the DEC-029 Lite edition
  • Guarded inventory location mapping and quantity operations
  • Fulfillment and carrier-tracking workflows
  • Controlled product export and update review
  • First-push, destructive-write, and notification safeguards
  • Readback, reconciliation, and manual-review evidence
  • Supported product, order, inventory, and fulfillment event acceleration

Full does not imply payouts, refunds, returns, billing, entitlement enforcement, or unlimited scale. Full supports up to ten configured Shopify stores per Odoo database, subject to readiness and capacity checks; this is a bounded support limit, not a throughput guarantee. Other future domains require their own accepted architecture and release gates.

Operationally explicit

Installation does not create a Shopify connection or transmit data. Configure a store and credentials in Odoo after installation, following the connector's setup guidance. Sensitive operations remain bounded and unsupported or ambiguous remote states are surfaced for review.
