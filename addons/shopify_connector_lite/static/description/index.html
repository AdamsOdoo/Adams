Shopify Connector Lite for Odoo 19

Reliable visibility into products, customers, and orders

The DEC-029 Lite edition installs the shared connector substrate plus product import and supported customer/order synchronization. It is the read-first edition for operators who want Shopify evidence and records available in Odoo without installing connector write-back domains.

Its installation closure includes the generic webhook foundation and separate product and order webhook accelerators. Supported events enter the existing asynchronous read jobs; scheduled reconciliation remains the loss-recovery backstop. No timing or exactly-once promise is made.

Lite is a thin meta-application. It adds no models, views, data records, scheduled actions, controllers, credentials, or Shopify requests of its own. The direct domain modules and webhook satellites remain independently identifiable and own their security and lifecycle rules.

Included edition scope

  • Store setup, readiness, jobs, logs, and operator recovery
  • Product and variant import with guarded matching
  • Customer import and supported order synchronization
  • Scheduled and manual read/reconciliation workflows
  • Supported product and order event acceleration
  • Explicit review for ambiguous or unsupported remote states
  • Durable run, retry, and recovery evidence

Lite contains no connector write-back modules. The edition boundary is module possession; it is not a billing, entitlement, or license-key mechanism. Standard Odoo behavior remains outside the connector edition boundary.

Operationally explicit

Installation does not create a Shopify connection or transmit data. Configure a store and credentials in Odoo after installation, following the connector's setup guidance. Unsupported or ambiguous states are surfaced for review rather than silently guessed.
